About Us

Plastic Patrol is a student-led project created by Year 7 students from TGG Campus. Our goal is to spread awareness about plastic pollution and inspire people to make eco-friendly choices for a cleaner and healthier future.

Who Are We?

We are a group of Year 7 students from TGG Campus working together to raise awareness about plastic pollution. Our website, Plastic Patrol, aims to educate people about the harmful effects of plastic waste on the environment, animals, and human health.

Through this project, we want to encourage everyone to reduce plastic usage, recycle properly, and protect our planet for future generations.

Our Goal

To raise awareness about plastic pollution and inspire positive environmental action.

Our Mission

To encourage people to reduce, reuse, and recycle plastic materials.

Our Vision

A cleaner and healthier future with less plastic pollution.

THE PROBLEM

Every year, millions of tons of plastic waste end up in our oceans, rivers, and landfills. This pollution harms marine life, damages ecosystems, and impacts human health.

But together, we can change this. Small actions today can lead to a big difference tomorrow.
