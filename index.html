CAUSES

Single-use plastics, littering, and improper waste disposal lead to plastic pollution.

Plastic harms marine life, pollutes oceans and rivers, and affects our health.

SOLUTIONS

Reduce, reuse, recycle and make eco-friendly choices every day.

What is Plastic Pollution?

Plastic pollution happens when plastic waste is thrown into the environment and does not break down easily. It pollutes oceans, rivers, streets, and forests, harming animals, marine life, and even human health. Many plastics take hundreds of years to decompose, which is why reducing plastic use is important for protecting our planet.

Why Should We Solve This Problem?

Plastic pollution damages our environment, kills wildlife, and pollutes oceans and rivers. If we do not take action, future generations may face serious environmental problems. By reducing plastic waste, we can protect animals, keep our communities clean, and create a healthier planet for everyone. If plastic pollution is not solved, some studies warn there could be more plastic than fish in the ocean by weight in certain areas by 2050, and microplastic contamination may become much worse.
