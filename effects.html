EFFECTS OF PLASTIC POLLUTION

Our Planet Is Suffering

Plastic pollution has serious effects on oceans, animals, ecosystems, and human health. Millions of tons of plastic waste damage the Earth every year and threaten the future of our planet.

HOW PLASTIC POLLUTION AFFECTS OUR WORLD

MARINE LIFE

Sea animals often mistake plastic for food, causing injury, starvation, and death.

ENVIRONMENT

Plastic waste pollutes beaches, rivers, forests, and damages ecosystems.

HUMAN HEALTH

Tiny plastic particles can enter food and water, affecting human health over time.

CLIMATE CHANGE

Plastic production and waste release harmful gases that contribute to climate change.

OUR OCEANS NEED HELP

Every year, millions of plastic items end up in oceans around the world. Marine animals become trapped in plastic waste or consume dangerous materials.

If we do not act now, plastic pollution will continue to grow and harm future generations.
